Questions

What are morning pages?

A popular journaling habit: write about three pages — roughly 750 words — by hand or on screen every morning, without editing. Inkstreak shows your words each morning, so you can see when you've hit it.

Does Inkstreak store my journal?

Only if you save an entry as a note in Inkstreak — then it stays on your device, in your Journal category. If you write in another app, Inkstreak only counts the words.

Can I journal by talking?

Yes. Say “Hey Siri, type in Inkstreak”, talk, then tap Stop. Your entry is saved as text under Write › Notes and the words count toward your streak.
