Support

Something not working? Check the answers below, or email support@inkstreak.app — include your device and iOS or macOS version.

Questions

How does Inkstreak count the words I type?

On iPhone and iPad you switch to the Inkstreak keyboard, which works in every app. It adds up words and characters as you type. On Mac, Inkstreak sits in the menu bar and counts typing in every app once you allow Input Monitoring.

Does Inkstreak read or save what I type?

No. The keyboard only adds up two numbers per day: words and characters. It never saves, reads back or sends your text, and password fields always switch to Apple's keyboard.

How do I cancel or restore my subscription?

Cancel anytime in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. On a new device, open Inkstreak › Setup › Restore. Your subscription is tied to your Apple Account, so there's no separate login.

The keyboard isn't counting.

Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Inkstreak › turn on Allow Full Access. Full Access only lets the keyboard pass the counts to the app.

“Hey Siri, type in Inkstreak” doesn't work.

Open Inkstreak once and allow the microphone and speech recognition (Write › Speak). Make sure Siri is on, and try saying “Ink Streak” as two words.

My Mac isn't counting.

System Settings › Privacy & Security › Input Monitoring › turn on Inkstreak, then quit and reopen it.

My devices don't add up.

Make sure every device is signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on. Totals can take a minute to appear.
