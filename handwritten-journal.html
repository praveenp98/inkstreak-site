Questions

How accurate is the word count?

Very good on printed text and neat handwriting, good on everyday handwriting, and lower on very messy cursive. A misread word still counts as a word, so the total is usually close.

Which languages can it read?

Handwriting works best in English and other languages using the Latin alphabet. Other scripts, including Hindi, are limited for now.

Does it save my words?

Only if you choose. By default Inkstreak saves the page photos and the count. Turn on “Keep the text” if you want to search your handwritten pages.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The pages are read on your iPhone with Apple's built-in text recognition. Nothing goes to us or anyone else.
