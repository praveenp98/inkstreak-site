👀 Notice

Small observations about right now.

  1. Describe the room you're in using only sounds.
  2. What's the first thing you noticed when you woke up today?
  3. Write about a stranger you saw recently. What story do you imagine for them?
  4. What colour has shown up most in your day so far?
  5. Describe your hands as if you were seeing them for the first time.
  6. What smell reminds you of a specific person?
  7. What's something in your home you walk past every day but never look at?
  8. Describe the light outside right now.
  9. What tiny thing went right today?
  10. Write down five things you can hear, four you can see, three you can touch.
  11. What did you eat today, and how did each thing actually taste?
  12. What's different about your street compared with a year ago?
  13. Describe the last conversation you overheard.
  14. What is the weather doing to your mood today?

🧠 Mind & patterns

How you think, and why.

  1. What thought has been looping in your head this week?
  2. When did you last change your mind about something important? What changed it?
  3. What story do you tell yourself when things go wrong?
  4. Which of your habits do you defend even though you know it doesn't help you?
  5. What do you avoid thinking about? What happens when you do think about it?
  6. Describe a time you overreacted. What was really going on underneath?
  7. What's a belief you inherited and never questioned?
  8. When do you feel most like yourself? Least?
  9. What does your inner critic sound like? Write down exactly what it says.
  10. Now answer your inner critic the way a kind friend would.
  11. What are you pretending not to know?
  12. What triggers you in other people that might be true of you too?
  13. How do you act when you feel unsafe? Where did you learn that?
  14. What would you do differently if no one was watching?
  15. Which emotion is hardest for you to show? Why?

💗 Feelings

Name it to tame it.

  1. How are you, really? Keep writing past the first answer.
  2. Where in your body do you feel today's mood?
  3. What are you excited about, even a little?
  4. What's weighing on you that you haven't said out loud?
  5. Write a letter to an emotion you're feeling right now.
  6. What made you laugh recently?
  7. What are you anxious about? What's the worst, best and most likely outcome?
  8. When did you last cry? What was it about?
  9. What does comfort look like for you today?
  10. What are you grieving — a person, a version of yourself, a plan?
  11. What made you angry this week? What did the anger want?
  12. Describe your mood as weather.

🫀 Body

Listen to the thing you live in.

  1. Scan from head to toe. What is each part telling you?
  2. How did you sleep, and how is it showing up today?
  3. What does your body need that you keep postponing?
  4. When does your body feel strongest?
  5. Write a thank-you note to a part of your body.
  6. What do you eat when you're stressed? What are you really hungry for?
  7. How does your body react before something scary?
  8. When did you last move just for fun?
  9. What's your energy like at 10am, 3pm and 10pm?
  10. What would you change about your day if you trusted your body completely?
  11. Describe the feeling of being truly rested.
  12. What did your body do today that you took for granted?

🌿 Nature & surroundings

The world outside your window.

  1. Go outside for two minutes. Write what you noticed.
  2. What's a tree, plant or animal you see often? Describe it closely.
  3. What season are you in — outside, and inside yourself?
  4. Where do you feel most at peace outdoors?
  5. Describe the sky right now.
  6. What does your neighbourhood sound like at night?
  7. What's a place in nature you return to in your memory?
  8. How has the climate changed where you live in your lifetime?
  9. If you could live anywhere on Earth for a year, where and why?
  10. Write about water — rain, sea, a river, a glass of it.
  11. What's the most beautiful thing you saw this month?

👥 People

Friends, family, love, everyone in between.

  1. Who made your week better? Have you told them?
  2. Describe someone you love without using their name.
  3. What conversation do you need to have but keep avoiding?
  4. Who do you miss? What would you say to them now?
  5. Which friendship has changed the most over the years?
  6. What did your parents get right? What would you do differently?
  7. Who do you envy, and what does that tell you about what you want?
  8. When did someone surprise you with kindness?
  9. What do people usually misunderstand about you?
  10. Write about the person you were closest to as a child.
  11. What boundaries do you need with someone in your life?
  12. Who would you call at 3am? Why them?
  13. Describe your ideal Sunday with the people you love.

🧒 Memories

Where you came from.

  1. Describe your childhood bedroom in detail.
  2. What's your earliest memory?
  3. What did you want to be when you grew up? What happened to that dream?
  4. Write about a smell, song or food that takes you back instantly.
  5. What's a family story everyone tells? What's the part no one tells?
  6. Describe a day from your past you'd love to live again.
  7. What rule from childhood do you still follow?
  8. Write about your first best friend.
  9. What were you afraid of as a kid? Are you still?
  10. What did a teacher say that stuck with you?
  11. Describe a festival or holiday as you remember it as a child.
  12. What's something you used to love and stopped doing?

🌱 Growth & habits

Who you're becoming.

  1. What's one thing you're better at than a year ago?
  2. What habit would make the biggest difference if you kept it for 30 days?
  3. What are you learning right now — on purpose or by accident?
  4. Describe a mistake that taught you something important.
  5. What are you ready to let go of?
  6. What would you try if you knew you couldn't fail?
  7. What does a good day look like for you, hour by hour?
  8. Where are you holding yourself back?
  9. What advice would you give yourself from five years ago?
  10. What does discipline mean to you? What does rest mean?
  11. What small promise could you keep to yourself today?
  12. What's a skill you admire in someone else? How could you start learning it?

💼 Work & purpose

What you do, and why it matters.

  1. What part of your work makes you lose track of time?
  2. What would you do all day if money didn't matter?
  3. What problem in the world do you wish you could fix?
  4. Describe a moment at work you felt proud.
  5. What drains your energy at work? What could change?
  6. What does success look like to you — not to anyone else?
  7. Who do you work best with, and why?
  8. What's a decision you're putting off?
  9. What are you building, in work or in life?
  10. If you had to teach one thing you know, what would it be?
  11. Write tomorrow's to-do list, then circle the one thing that truly matters.

💰 Money

Your relationship with it.

  1. What did your family teach you about money, out loud and silently?
  2. What's the best thing you've ever spent money on?
  3. What purchase do you regret? Why?
  4. How much is enough? Try to put a number on it.
  5. What do you spend on to feel better?
  6. What would change in your day if money stress disappeared?
  7. What does being rich mean to you, beyond money?
  8. Write about a time you felt generous.
  9. What's one money habit you want to start this month?

🌍 The world

Society, culture, the news, the future.

  1. What news story has stayed with you this week? Why that one?
  2. What's something everyone around you believes that you're not sure about?
  3. Which tradition from your culture do you love? Which would you change?
  4. What will people in 100 years find strange about how we live now?
  5. How has technology changed your relationships?
  6. What community do you belong to? What do you give it?
  7. If you could ask a world leader one question, what would it be?
  8. What's a book, film or song that changed how you see the world?
  9. What does fairness mean to you? Where do you see it missing?
  10. Describe your town to someone who will never visit.
  11. What gives you hope about the future?

🔭 Big questions

Philosophy for your notebook.

  1. What do you think happens when we die? Has your answer changed?
  2. What makes a life well lived?
  3. Are you the same person you were ten years ago? What stayed the same?
  4. Is it better to be happy or to be good?
  5. What do you know for certain?
  6. What would you want written about you in 100 years?
  7. Do you believe in luck, fate, or choice? Give an example from your life.
  8. What is time to you — a river, a circle, a resource?
  9. If you could know one truth about the universe, what would you ask?
  10. What do you worship — not religiously, but in practice?
  11. Where does your sense of meaning come from?
  12. What does freedom mean to you right now?

🌙 Dreams & the unconscious

Night-time clues.

  1. Write down last night's dream, even the fragments.
  2. What dream do you keep having? What might it be asking of you?
  3. Pick a symbol from a recent dream. What does it mean to you, not to a dream dictionary?
  4. Describe a daydream you return to often.
  5. What do you think about just before you fall asleep?
  6. If your intuition could speak in one sentence today, what would it say?
  7. Write about a coincidence that felt meaningful.
  8. What part of yourself do you only see when you're tired?
  9. Draw (in words) the house you lived in during a dream.

✨ Astrology

Use the stars as a mirror for reflection.

  1. Your sun sign is said to describe your core self. What fits you — and what doesn't?
  2. Your moon sign is linked to emotions and comfort. What actually comforts you?
  3. Your rising sign is the 'first impression'. How do strangers usually see you?
  4. Which element do you feel most — fire, earth, air or water? Why?
  5. During the next Mercury retrograde, what would you like to slow down and review?
  6. New moon: what do you want to begin this month?
  7. Full moon: what has come to light or reached its peak lately?
  8. Saturn is linked to discipline and lessons. What lesson keeps returning in your life?
  9. Venus is linked to love and values. What do you value most in relationships?
  10. Mars is linked to drive. What gets you moving — and what stops you?
  11. Read today's horoscope for your sign. Which line stuck with you, and why?
  12. If your life were a birth chart, which 'house' needs attention right now?

🧬 Human Design

Reflect on your type, strategy and authority.

  1. Your Human Design type (Generator, Projector, Manifestor, Reflector…) — which parts ring true for you?
  2. Generators are said to follow a gut 'yes'. What did your gut say yes to this week?
  3. Projectors wait for recognition. When did you last feel truly seen?
  4. Manifestors are told to inform before acting. Who would you like to keep in the loop?
  5. Reflectors are said to sample the energy around them. Which place or person changes your mood most?
  6. What does your 'not-self' feeling look like — frustration, bitterness, anger, disappointment?
  7. Your authority is how you're meant to decide. How did you make your last big decision?
  8. Where do you spend energy that isn't really yours to spend?
  9. When do you feel most aligned — rested, clear and energised at once?
  10. Which open centre in your chart do you relate to? What do you absorb from others?

🔮 Intuition & symbols

Tarot, signs, gut feelings.

  1. Pull a tarot card (or imagine one). What story does it tell about your week?
  2. What sign have you been waiting for? What would you do if it came today?
  3. When did your gut feeling turn out to be right?
  4. Pick a number that keeps appearing for you. Where have you seen it?
  5. If your life had a symbol right now, what would it be?
  6. Describe a ritual — big or tiny — that grounds you.
  7. What would your wisest future self whisper to you today?
  8. Choose a colour for this month. Why that one?

🙏 Gratitude

Three good things, and more.

  1. Three good things from today, however small.
  2. Who are you grateful for that you rarely thank?
  3. What's something you have now that you once wished for?
  4. What simple pleasure do you never get tired of?
  5. Write about a hard time that gave you something good.
  6. What about your body are you thankful for today?
  7. Which place are you grateful exists?
  8. What modern convenience would you miss most?
  9. Describe a meal you're grateful for.

🎨 Creativity

Play with words.

  1. Write a six-word story about your day.
  2. Describe your morning as the opening of a novel.
  3. Write a letter from your phone to you.
  4. Invent a holiday. What do people do on it?
  5. Write a poem about the last thing you ate.
  6. Describe a colour to someone who can't see.
  7. Write the conversation two objects on your desk would have.
  8. Start with 'The door was already open…' and keep going for 5 minutes.
  9. Rewrite a bad moment from this week as a comedy.
  10. What would your life's soundtrack be? List the songs and why.
  11. Describe your city in the year 2080.

🕰️ Future you

Plans, hopes, letters forward.

  1. Write a letter to yourself one year from today.
  2. Describe an ordinary day five years from now.
  3. What do you want to be known for?
  4. What's one thing you'll be glad you started today?
  5. If this year had a title, what would you want it to be?
  6. What would make next month feel like a good month?
  7. Who do you want to become? What would that person do today?
  8. What fear do you hope future you has outgrown?
  9. List ten things you want to do before you die.

🌧️ Hard days

For when it's heavy. Just let it out.

  1. Write everything you're feeling without fixing it. Just let it out.
  2. What's the hardest part of today? What's one thing that helped, even slightly?
  3. What would you say to a friend going through exactly this?
  4. What do you need right now — rest, help, space, or someone to listen?
  5. List what's in your control and what isn't.
  6. What has got you through hard times before?
  7. Write the thing you can't say to anyone. You don't have to keep it.
  8. What's one gentle thing you can do for yourself in the next hour?

📍 Places & travel

Where you've been and where you'd go.

  1. Describe the best trip you've ever taken, one moment at a time.
  2. What place changed you?
  3. Write about getting lost somewhere.
  4. What's a place you'll never go back to? Why?
  5. Where would you go tomorrow if you could?
  6. What does 'home' mean — a place, people, a feeling?
  7. Describe a street from your past so someone could walk it.
  8. What's the most interesting conversation you had with a stranger while travelling?

Questions

How do I use journal prompts?

Pick one that catches your eye and write for five minutes without stopping. Don't edit — just follow the thought. If a prompt doesn't fit today, skip it.

How often should I journal?

A little every day beats a lot once a week. Even three sentences keep the habit alive — that's why Inkstreak counts a streak, not a word goal.

Are the astrology and Human Design prompts predictions?

No. They use astrology and Human Design as a mirror for reflection — questions to help you notice what's true for you.
