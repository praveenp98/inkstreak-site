How to export a WhatsApp chat (30 seconds)

Best pick: the chat with yourself (Message yourself), or any chat where you write a lot. Only your own messages are counted.

iPhone

  1. Open the chat and tap the name at the top.
  2. Scroll down and tap Export Chat.
  3. Choose Without Media, then Save to Files.
  4. Come back here and choose that file.

Android

  1. Open the chat and tap ⋮ at the top right.
  2. Tap More › Export chat › Without media.
  3. Save it to Drive or your Files app.
  4. Come back here and choose that file.
