What your words can do for you

Once you've written 10,000 words, your notes hold patterns you can't see from inside them. An AI can read them back to you: what you keep returning to, what you avoid, what you've already figured out. You still do the writing. The AI just holds up the mirror.

Four rules first

AI reads, you write

Ask it to quote and reflect your words. Never ask it to rewrite your journal or write the next entry for you.

Remove what isn't yours to share

Delete other people's full names, phone numbers, addresses and anything someone told you in confidence before you paste.

Turn off training first

Most AI apps can learn from your chats unless you switch it off. Steps for each app are below.

Treat the answer as a guess

An AI notices patterns but doesn't know you. Keep what rings true, ignore the rest, and write down what you disagree with.

Before you paste: keep your notes out of AI training

Inkstreak never sends your words anywhere; the buttons on this page only copy them to your clipboard. What happens after you paste depends on the app you paste into.

ChatGPT
  1. Tap your profile picture, then Settings.
  2. Open Data Controls.
  3. Turn off Improve the model for everyone.

Or start a Temporary Chat, which isn't used for training.

Claude
  1. Open Settings, then Privacy.
  2. Turn off Help improve our AI models.

New chats after that aren't used for training.

Gemini
  1. In the Gemini app, tap your profile picture.
  2. Open Gemini Apps Activity (Google is renaming it Keep Activity).
  3. Tap Turn off.

Google may still keep chats for up to 72 hours to run the service.

Most private: an AI on your own computer
  1. Install a free app such as LM Studio or Ollama on a laptop.
  2. Download a model in the app.
  3. Paste your notes there. Nothing leaves your computer.

Answers are a bit less sharp than the big apps, but your words never touch the internet.

Steps checked Nov 6, 2026: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini. Apps change their menus; if a name differs, look under privacy or data settings.
