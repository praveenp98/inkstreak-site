Big assignments are just small days

A 10,000-word dissertation is 500 words a day for 20 days. Inkstreak makes that visible: write wherever you write and see today's words next to yesterday's. Miss a day and your streak tells you.

How students use Inkstreak

  • Thesis or dissertation: tag your writing “Thesis” on the keyboard so it's counted separately.
  • Essays and assignments: see how much you actually wrote today, not how long you sat at the desk.
  • Exam season: practise answers every day and keep a streak going until the last paper.
  • Brainstorming: talk through ideas with voice to text, then copy the note into your draft.

Private by design

Inkstreak never stores what you type — only the number of words and characters per day. There's no account and no tracking.

Questions

Can Inkstreak tell me how many words my essay has?

Inkstreak counts the words you write each day, not the length of one document. Use it to see your daily progress on an essay or thesis; use your writing app's word count for the final total.

Is it allowed in exams or at school?

Inkstreak only counts words; it doesn't write, check or change anything. Follow your school's rules about phones and keyboards.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Counting works without internet. Voice to text needs internet once to download Apple's speech model, then works offline.
