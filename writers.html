A plan for 50,000 words in November

National Novel Writing Month may have closed, but November is still novel month for thousands of writers. Here's a simple plan with Inkstreak:

  1. Aim for about 1,700 words a day — Inkstreak shows exactly where you are.
  2. On the keyboard, tap the tag button and pick Novel so your book is counted separately from messages.
  3. Short on time? Go for a walk and say “Hey Siri, type in Inkstreak”. Draft out loud — the words count.
  4. Don't break the streak. Even 100 words keeps it alive.

Questions

How many words a day do I need for 50,000 words in November?

1,667 words a day for 30 days. Inkstreak shows your words today and compares them with yesterday, so you always know if you're on pace.

Does Inkstreak work with Scrivener, Word, Pages or Google Docs?

Yes. On iPhone and iPad, use the Inkstreak keyboard inside any writing app. On Mac, the menu-bar counter counts typing in every app and shows each one by name.

Can I track only my novel, not my messages?

Yes. Tap the tag button on the keyboard and pick “Novel”. Everything you type is filed under that tag until you switch back, so the Apps tab shows your novel separately.
