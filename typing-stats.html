Why iPhone shows types of apps, not app names

Apple doesn't tell keyboards which app they're in — a privacy rule we agree with. So Inkstreak sorts by the kind of text box instead (a chat, a search bar, a document), or by a tag you pick. On Mac you get real app names.

Questions

How many words do I type a day?

It varies a lot from person to person — the only way to know is to measure. Inkstreak counts every word you type on iPhone, iPad and Mac and shows your daily total, your average and your best day.

Does Inkstreak show typing speed (WPM)?

Not yet. Inkstreak focuses on how much you write: words, characters, where, and your streak.

Is this a keylogger?

No. Inkstreak never records which keys you press or what you write — it only adds up totals per day. On Mac it ignores passwords and keyboard shortcuts.
