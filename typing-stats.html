Questions

Does the keyboard save what I type?

No. It only adds up how many words and characters you type. It never stores, reads back or sends your text, and makes no network requests.

Why does it need Full Access?

Only so the keyboard can pass two numbers (today's words and characters) to the Inkstreak app. It works as a keyboard without it.
