Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Inkstreak does not collect, store or share anything you type.

What Inkstreak keeps

For each day, the number of words and characters you typed, grouped by the kind of text box or tag (on iPhone and iPad) or by app name (on Mac). These numbers are stored on your device.

Sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, these daily numbers are synced between your own devices using Apple's iCloud. They are not sent to us or anyone else.

Voice notes

When you record a voice note, your speech is turned into text on your device by Apple's on-device speech recognition. The text is saved on your device only, and the audio itself is not kept. The microphone is used only while you are recording. You can delete any note at any time.

Optional: more accurate voice-to-text. On iPhones running iOS 18–25 you can turn on “More accurate voice-to-text” in Setup. Only then, while you record, your audio is sent to Apple's speech recognition service to be turned into text, under Apple's privacy policy. It never goes to us, and it's off unless you turn it on.

Notes

Anything you type or speak inside Inkstreak and save as a note is stored on your device only, in the category you choose. You can delete any note at any time.

What Inkstreak never keeps

The text you type in other apps, which keys you press, passwords, or any personal information. The keyboard makes no network connections.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions and free trials are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. Inkstreak never sees your name, email or payment details — the app only checks with the App Store whether a subscription is active.

Streak buddies

If you invite a friend, Inkstreak shares a name and emoji you choose, your streak length and today's word count with the people who open your link — never your notes or anything you type. It is stored in your own iCloud and shared through Apple's iCloud sharing; we don't run a server and never receive it. You can stop sharing at any time in Stats › Streak buddies, and old links stop working.

Analytics and ads

None.

Deleting your data

Use Setup › Reset in the app, delete individual voice notes, or delete the app.
