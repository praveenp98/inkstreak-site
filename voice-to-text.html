Talk. It becomes text.

Ideas don't wait for a keyboard. With Inkstreak you can record a voice note from the Lock Screen and get clean text back — saved by day, searchable, and ready to copy into your draft, email or chat.

Three ways to start

  • Siri: “Hey Siri, type in Inkstreak.” Say “Hey Siri, stop Inkstreak” or tap Stop to save.
  • Action Button: Settings › Action Button › Shortcut › Inkstreak › Voice note.
  • In the app: Write › Speak, tap the big microphone.

On your device, not in the cloud

Inkstreak uses Apple's on-device speech engine. There's no account and no upload, and the audio is discarded once it's text.

Spoken words count, too

Every voice note adds to your words today and keeps your writing streak alive. Prefer to count typing only? Turn it off in Setup.

Questions

Is the voice to text private?

Yes. Inkstreak uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your voice is turned into text on your iPhone; the audio isn't kept and nothing is uploaded.

What's the fastest way to start a voice note?

Add the Inkstreak Voice note button to your Lock Screen, Control Center or Action Button, or make your own Siri phrase like “Hey Siri, quick note”. Inkstreak opens and starts listening; do it again to save. If you lock your phone while talking, a banner on the Lock Screen keeps the timer and a Stop button.

How long can a voice note be?

Up to an hour. Notes are saved automatically when you stop, when a call comes in, or after an hour.

Which iPhones are supported?

iPhones running iOS 26 or later. The Action Button needs iPhone 15 Pro or newer.
