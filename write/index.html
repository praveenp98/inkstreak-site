Add a paper page

Wrote in a notebook? Turn the page into text with your phone's own scanner. The words count toward today, and nothing is uploaded.

0 words
How to scan on iPhone
  1. Easiest: open the Camera app and point it at your page. When a yellow frame appears, tap the Live Text button (lines inside a frame, bottom right), tap Copy All, come back here and tap 📋 Paste.
  2. Or right here: tap the box above, tap it again, choose AutoFill › Scan Text (on older iPhones just Scan Text), point at the page and tap Insert.
  3. Already have a photo of the page? Open it in Photos, press and hold the text, tap Select All › Copy, then Paste here.
How to scan on Android
  1. Open Google Lens (or the camera's Lens button) and point at the page.
  2. Tap Text, then Select all › Copy.
  3. Come back here and tap 📋 Paste.

Fix any words it misread. Prefer not to scan? Close this and use Log words with a rough count.
