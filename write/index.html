Add a paper page

Choose a photo of your page and the text is read right on your phone. Nothing is uploaded. Fix any words it gets wrong, then save.

0 words · fix any words it misread

How to get the text on iPhone
  1. Tap Choose a photo of the page, pick it from your Photos (or take one).
  2. When the photo appears, press and hold the writing in it, tap Select All, then Copy.
  3. Tap 📋 Paste text. Safari asks once; tap Paste.

No photo yet? Open the Camera app, point at the page, tap the Live Text button (lines in a frame, bottom right), tap Copy All, come back and tap Paste text.

How to get the text on Android
  1. Open the photo of your page in Google Photos or your Gallery and tap Lens (or open the Google Lens app and point at the page).
  2. Tap Text, then Select all and Copy.
  3. Come back and tap 📋 Paste text.

Choose the photo here too if you'd like to keep it with the note.

The photo stays on this device only and isn't in backups. Prefer not to scan? Close this and use Log words on the Progress tab with a rough count.
